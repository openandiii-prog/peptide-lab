Chapter 3 · Metabolic data

Weight-loss scoreboard

Mean body-weight change in each drug's pivotal trial at its top dose. Different trials, different lengths and populations, so read it as a map of the field, not a head-to-head race.

Sources: STEP-1 (semaglutide), SURMOUNT-1 (tirzepatide), TRIUMPH-1 (retatrutide, 2026), REDEFINE-1 (CagriSema and cagrilintide), SCALE (liraglutide), ATTAIN-1 (orforglipron, a small-molecule pill approved April 2026, shown for comparison), setmelanotide phase 3 in POMC deficiency (rare genetic obesity). In the head-to-head REDEFINE-4, CagriSema reached 23.0% vs 25.5% for tirzepatide and missed non-inferiority.

Chapter 4 · Pharmacokinetics

How long each one lasts

Plasma half-life on a log scale, from two minutes (native GLP-1) to almost two months (a degarelix depot). Nearly every long-acting drug here got there with one of three tricks: unnatural D-amino acids, a fatty-acid tail that rides on albumin, or a depot under the skin.
